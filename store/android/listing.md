## Full description (4,000 characters max)

```
The world ended on the day I came of age. This is what we built after.

Afterlight is a story-driven JRPG in the 2D-HD style: pixel-art heroes in lit, miniature dioramas. You are Wren, sixteen, on the day she is meant to receive her Crest, the calling that decides a whole life, in a world where seven Lanterns sing the sun back into the sky every morning. Then the sun breaks into seven pieces, and it is her fault.

What follows is a long road across Solmere, before and after it breaks: a port town under curfew, a city of four hundred bells, a desert of glass, a marsh where the light went out, and the islands left hanging in the sky when everything fell.

A FULL-LENGTH STORY
• 14 chapters in three acts, two endings, and a 30-floor post-game descent.
• Six party members with their own voices, and side quests about the people you meet.
• Characters react to who is in the party and what you have done.

BATTLES YOU CAN READ
• A turn-order timeline shows where each command will put your next turn.
• Find weaknesses, Break a foe's Poise, and Guard the attacks bosses wind up for.
• Spend the shared Afterglow gauge on Surge, or on Link Arts between two or three heroes.
• Swap party members mid-fight, or hand a fight to auto-battle.

BUILD YOUR PARTY
• 28 Crests (classes) with 10 ranks each, and more than 300 Arts.
• Give every hero a primary and a secondary Crest, and equip Supports on a point budget.
• Master a Crest to keep its Legacy whatever you wear next.

A WORLD TO WALK
• 145 hand-laid maps, a world map with fast travel, and a Quest Guide that marks where to go.
• Fishing, a bounty board, three arenas, optional dungeons, a mount, and New Game+.

MADE FOR A PHONE
• On-screen controls, or tap to advance a conversation.
• Fast Mode, hold-to-skip for scenes you have seen, a backlog of the last hundred lines, and autosave.
• Plays offline. No ads, no purchases, no account, and nothing is collected.

Every sprite, texture and piece of music in Afterlight is generated by the game itself.
```

About 2,000 characters.
