# AFTERLIGHT — Story Bible

> *"The world ended on the day I came of age. This is what we built after."*

## Premise

Solmere is a world lit by the **Hearthsun**. Every evening the sun sinks into the Western
Sea; every dawn the **Seven Lanterns** — ancient spire-lighthouses tended by **Keepers** —
sing it back into the sky with the **Dawnsong**. Beyond the edge of the world lies the
**Gloam**: the formless dark where forgotten memories drift and sour into monsters.

At sixteen, every child of Solmere undergoes **the Kindling**: they stand in a Lantern's
light and receive a **Crest**, a calling etched into the soul — Vanguard, Mender, Arcanist…
Society holds to an old proverb: *"One Crest, one life."* You are what the light says you are.

AFTERLIGHT is the story of children who are told who to be, lose the world that told them,
and decide for themselves who they will become.

## Themes

1. **Growing up means accepting loss.** You cannot go home again; you can build a new one.
2. **The adults you idolize are people.** Heroes fail. Villains grieve.
3. **Guilt vs. responsibility.** Guilt looks backward; responsibility looks forward.
4. **You are not one thing.** The Crest system *is* the theme: the party breaks "one Crest,
   one life" and becomes many things at once.
5. **The comfort of the past is a cage.** The antagonist wants every good day to never end.

## Cast

### Wren Halloway (16) — protagonist
Daughter of the Keeper of Lanternfall, a tiny fishing isle at the western edge of the world.
Restless, curious, quick to laugh and quicker to leap. Reads every book that washes ashore.
Father **Edrin** was lost at sea when she was nine; she secretly believes her mother
**Ysolde** could have saved him. She idolizes **Cassian**, the Hierophant, who writes her
letters and calls her "a restless heart, like mine."
- **Kindling Crest:** Vanguard. **Personal skill:** *Resolve* — Afterglow gain +25%, and
  survives one lethal blow per battle at 1 HP.
- **Arc:** From wanting to escape, to betraying her mother's trust and causing the end of
  the world, to carrying that weight without letting it crush her, to choosing the future
  over the past — even when the past is offered back to her with her mother's face.

### Ilse Marrow (16) — the healer
Wren's best friend since they were toddlers. Apprentice to the village herbalist, raised by
her grandmother **Nana Brisa**. Dry wit, careful, a rule-follower. Plans everything.
- **Kindling Crest:** Mender. **Personal skill:** *Triage* — healing on allies below 30% HP
  is +50%.
- **Arc:** She always did what was expected. After the Sundering she becomes the hardened
  leader of the Ember Ward, a field hospital in the ruins of Aurenne. She is furious with
  Wren. Her growth is learning she is allowed to want things for herself — and choosing to
  forgive, not because Wren deserves it, but because Ilse does.

### Bram Kestrel (19) — the shield
A squire of the **Dawnguard** who froze in terror during his knighting trial; his mentor
**Sir Aldous Venn** died covering for him. Exiled to "guard" Lanternfall. Loud, boastful,
terrible at cards, kind to children and animals, secretly certain he is a coward.
- **Kindling Crest:** Warden. **Personal skill:** *Bulwark* — when an ally would be hit by
  a lethal blow, Bram may intercept it (once per battle).
- **Arc:** Found after the timeskip drinking himself numb as a sellsword in Kiteholm. Faces
  **the Penitent** — the Gloam-revived husk of Sir Aldous. Learns courage is not the absence
  of fear but acting while afraid.

### Sable (appears 16) — the girl from the sea
Washes ashore during the Kindling-eve storm with no memory and a black Crest no Lantern
recognizes: **Gloamweaver**. Literal, curious, delighted by small things (bread, rain,
cats). Speaks in short sentences. Learns humor by watching Pip.
- **Truth:** She is a *Remembrance* — a body woven from the Gloam by Cassian, shaped from his
  memory of his lost sister **Lysa**, meant as a vessel for Lysa's return in Evernoon. She
  fled him. She has Lysa's face.
- **Personal skill:** *Gloamtouch* — her attacks may inflict a random status; she is immune
  to Doom.
- **Arc:** The most literal coming-of-age: in Evernoon she would stay sixteen forever. She
  chooses to be **Sable**, not Lysa — chooses a name, a future, and the right to grow old.

### Pip (Pipkin Tallow) (11 → 12) — the urchin
A pickpocket from the gutters of Aurenne who steals Wren's compass, gets caught, and refuses
to leave. Fast-talking, funny, desperate to be treated as grown.
- **Kindling Crest:** none at first — too young. Receives an illegal early Kindling from the
  blind Keeper Tamsin in Saltglass: Rogue. **Personal skill:** *Pilfer* — steals on every
  basic attack (low rate).
- **Arc:** After the timeskip, twelve-year-old Pip leads twenty orphans in the shanty-town of
  Kiteholm, hard-eyed and adult. The party gives him back permission to be a kid.

### Corvin Ashe (34) — the sky-pirate
Captain of the airship *Halcyon*, smuggler, gambler, charming coward. A Dawnguard deserter
who once owed Cassian his life.
- **Crest:** Rogue (originally Vanguard). **Personal skill:** *Gambit* — 1 in 6 chance any
  of his skills costs no MP and deals double.
- **Arc:** The adult who never grew up becomes the father figure the children need. His
  defining moment: staying when everything in him says run.

### Cassian Lorne (52) — the Hierophant
Leader of the Choir, Keeper of the Grand Lantern of Aurenne. Gentle, brilliant, beloved by
the whole world. When he was twelve, a Lantern failure let the Gloam spill into Aurenne for
one night; his ten-year-old sister **Lysa** was taken. His father, the Keeper, chose to save
the Lantern instead of her. Cassian has spent forty years composing the **Vespersong** — the
Dawnsong sung backwards through all Seven Lanterns at once — to stop the sun at a single
perfect moment and pull Lysa back out of the Gloam's memory.
- He does not believe he is a villain. *"I am giving the world what every child wants: for
  the good days never to end."*
- **Truth he refuses:** a world that cannot change cannot live. The Vespersong does not
  freeze the world — it shatters it.

### Ysolde Halloway — Keeper of Lanternfall
Wren's mother. Stern, overprotective, privately terrified of losing the only family she has
left. Suspects Cassian. Dies holding Lanternfall's light during the Sundering so Wren can
escape. Her last words: *"Grow up well, my little bird."*

### Antagonists' agents — the Vespers
- **Sister Mercy Vale** — a fanatic Cantor who believes Evernoon will end all suffering.
  Experiments on the poor of Aurenne's Undercroft. Recurring boss.
- **Lord-Commander Garrick Thorne** — head of the Dawnguard, Cassian's sword. Brutal,
  pragmatic, contemptuous of Bram. Recurring boss.
- **The Penitent** — a masked knight in black armor: the Gloam-revived husk of Sir Aldous Venn.
- **The Remnants** — what the Keepers become after the Sundering: their memories twisted
  around the seven Sun Shards.

## Plot

### ACT I — DAWN (≈ 18 hours)

**Ch.1 "The Eve of Kindling."** Lanternfall Isle, the day before Wren's sixteenth birthday.
Errands (tutorial): nets for old Fisher Tobias, herbs with Ilse, a dare into the Tide Caves
(first dungeon) to recover Ilse's grandmother's brooch. Cassian's ship arrives — the
Hierophant himself has come for the Kindling, the first time in decades. He remembers
Wren's letters. That night a storm; a girl washes up on the black rocks pursued by
Gloam-hounds. Wren and Ilse fight (first boss: **the Drowned Hound**). Bram arrives late,
heroically. The girl wakes and has no name; Wren names her **Sable** for her black hair.

**Ch.2 "The Kindling."** The ceremony. Wren receives **Vanguard**, Ilse **Mender**. When Sable
steps into the light, the Lantern *gutters* and a black Crest no one recognizes burns on her
hand. Cassian goes white — Sable has his dead sister's face. That night Wren overhears
Cassian asking Ysolde for the Lanternfall **Dawnstone** "for maintenance at the Cathedral."
Ysolde refuses. Next morning Cassian asks Wren to escort Sable to Aurenne "where the Choir's
archives can help her," with Ilse and Bram. Ysolde forbids it. Wren goes anyway. Ysolde, at
the dock, does not stop her — just gives her Edrin's old compass. Sea crossing (mini-boss:
**Brinefang**, a sea serpent).

**Ch.3 "The Wider World."** Port Gale. The Whisperwold, where the trees speak in the voices
of the dead (foreshadowing the Gloam). Thistledown, where the **Crestless Sage** — an old
woman who has *five* Crests — teaches the forbidden art of changing your Crest (**Crest
system unlocks fully**). Highwayman **Red Tam** and his gang (boss). First Vesper sighting:
Garrick's soldiers hunting "a black-haired girl."

**Ch.4 "City of Bells."** Aurenne. **Pip** steals Wren's compass; rooftop chase; he joins.
Cassian receives them warmly and offers Wren her dream: to be his **Lantern Page**. Sable
dreams of a boy calling her "Lysa." People vanish in the Undercroft — the party descends and
finds **Sister Mercy** making Gloam-beasts from the poor (boss). Mercy escapes. Cassian
expresses horror, dismisses her publicly, and gives Wren a mission: carry blessed **tuning
forks** to the other Keepers so the Lanterns can be "harmonized" against rising Gloam.

**Ch.5 "The Pilgrim Road."** Sunscar Desert: Keeper **Tamsin** is blind and sees more than
anyone; she gives Pip an illegal early Kindling (Rogue). Wrecked airship *Halcyon*: **Corvin
Ashe** joins after a sand-wyrm fight. Greyspine: **Brother Halvard** examines the fork and
grows afraid — "this is not tuned to the Dawnsong; it is tuned to its *reflection*."
Garrick ambushes them; Halvard dies in an avalanche he triggers to save them. Mirefen:
**Mother Osk**, sick with Gloam-rot. Verdance: ancient **Elowen** tells the story of
the night forty years ago — the Gloam spilled into Aurenne, and the Keeper's daughter, Lysa
Lorne, was taken. Sable has Lysa's face. Skyreach: **Ardent Vasha** has already given her
Dawnstone to Cassian. Six of seven are his.

**Ch.6 "The Last Sunset."** The party races home. Cassian is waiting at Lanternfall. He does
not lie. He tells Wren everything: the Vespersong, Evernoon, a world where no one dies again —
where her father is pulled back out of the Gloam and the good days never end. *"Your mother
let him go, Wren. She will let you go too, someday. I never will."* Ysolde refuses. Wren —
sixteen, grieving, furious at her mother — takes the Dawnstone from the Lantern's heart and
gives it to him. Ilse begs her not to. Sable screams. At sunset the Vespersong is sung. The sun
stops… and then cracks into seven pieces. The sea falls upward. Ysolde climbs the Lantern and
holds Lanternfall's last light long enough for the children to get to the boat. Mother and
daughter. *"I'm sorry." / "Grow up well, my little bird."* White.

> **Title card: AFTERLIGHT**

### ACT II — DUSK (≈ 17 hours)

**Ch.7 "Afterlight."** One year later. Wren wakes on a floating shard of what was Lanternfall,
nursed by a hermit, **Old Fennick**. The sky is a permanent bruise of twilight with seven
burning wounds where the sun used to be. Wren is alone, and the world is ash, glass, and
floating islands over an abyss that used to be the sea. She learns to fight again, alone,
and crosses the **Shattered Sea** by sky-bridges and wreckage.

**Ch.8 "The Mender of Ash."** The ruins of Aurenne. Ilse runs the **Ember Ward**, a field
hospital for the Gloam-rot. She is cold, efficient, and will not look at Wren. Medicine lies
in the drowned Undercroft. Ilse rejoins — *"For the patients. Not for you."*

**Ch.9 "Children of the Wreck."** Kiteholm, a shanty town of kites and scrap. Pip leads
twenty orphans. Bram is a hired sword, drunk. Vesper raiders led by **the Penitent** attack;
Bram freezes, then — when Pip is struck — fights. The Penitent unmasks: Sir Aldous's face,
hollow. Cassian's Evernoon is real, and full of puppets wearing the dead.

**Ch.10 "The Halcyon."** Corvin's grounded airship, his debts, his hiding. Rebuilding the
ship (multi-part quest across the Ruin). The party has wings again.

**Ch.11 "Lysa."** The **Noon Spire** on the greatest Sun Shard. Inside: Evernoon — a perfect
Aurenne, frozen on a summer festival afternoon forty years ago. Sable, in Lysa's dress,
half-dissolved into the memory. Sister Mercy (boss). Cassian, gentle as ever, offers
everyone their dead back. Sable chooses her name. Cassian: *"Then I will need all seven
Shards."* Escape.

### ACT III — DAWN AGAIN (≈ 15 hours + post-game)

**Ch.12 "Seven Wounds."** Open world by airship. Seven Sun Shards, each held by a **Remnant**:
the Blind Choir (Tamsin), the Frozen Penitence (Halvard), the Mire Mother (Osk), the Withered
Bloom (Elowen), the Storm Herald (Vasha), Garrick Thorne (alive, in the Grand Lantern), and at
Lanternfall… **the Keeper's Remnant** — a memory of Ysolde. Wren must fight her mother's
shadow, and in winning receives the **Lanternkeeper** Crest. Each companion has a personal
**Rite** quest yielding their ultimate weapon and legendary Crest.

**Ch.13 "Evernoon."** The final dungeon: Cassian's frozen memory of childhood. The party
walks through the night Lysa was lost and meets twelve-year-old Cassian. Final battles:
**Cassian, the Hierophant → the Noon King → Vesper, the Unending Moment.** Before the last
phase Cassian offers Wren Lanternfall restored, her mother alive, the day before her
Kindling — forever. The player chooses.

- **Accept (Evernoon ending):** The party smiles at the Kindling-eve festival. The day repeats.
  It is beautiful and it never ends. "THE END?" — a gentle horror.
- **Refuse (true ending):** continue to the final phase.

**Ch.14 "Afterlight."** The seven Shards cannot remake the old sun. Instead the party sings
something new — the **Afterlight** — and kindles a smaller, stranger dawn. Cassian, finally
grieving, lets Sable hold his hand as Evernoon dissolves. Epilogue: rebuilding; a new
Kindling where children *choose* their Crests; Ilse's hospital; Bram teaching kids to hold
a sword and to be afraid; Pip, a child again; Corvin, a father; Sable, getting older. And
Wren, leaving the island at last — the thing she wanted at the start, now earned.

### POST-GAME
- **The Gloam Abyss** — 30-floor procedural descent; floor 30: the **First Dark**.
- **Colosseum of Kiteholm** — ranked battles.
- Crest mastery for all 24 Crests; legendary Crests: **Sunforged**, **Afterlight**.

## Tone guide for dialogue

- Short lines. Subtext over exposition. Let characters be funny when it's dark.
- Wren: fast, warm, a little too honest. Uses "okay" when she isn't.
- Ilse: dry, precise, lists. *"Three things. One, you're bleeding. Two—"*
- Bram: big words used slightly wrong; heart on sleeve.
- Sable: short, literal. *"Bread is warm. I did not know food could be warm."*
- Pip: street slang, bravado, sudden kid moments.
- Corvin: drawl, deflection, endearments ("kid", "sparrow").
- Cassian: kind, measured, never raises his voice. Always reasonable. That is the horror.
